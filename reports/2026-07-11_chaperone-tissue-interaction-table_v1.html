Alternatives considered · Gaps · Agents

Alternatives considered
  • Single-signal model (RA only): rejected as incomplete — RA patterns proximo-distal identity but does not establish the posterior competence field (Wnt/FGF) or the DV origin (BMP).
  • Notch as an inducing source signal: rejected for this table — Notch (e.g. jag2b/deltaC) acts within the pronephros for multiciliated-vs-epithelial cell-type patterning, not as a source→competent-tissue inducer; belongs to a later cell-fate table.
  • Treating pax2a/osr1/wt1 as "signals": rejected — these are target-intrinsic response TFs (readout of competence), separated into the companion module.
Human gate (Hard Rule §7). The sufficiency column and the source→signal→window structure are a causal-pruner-class induction hypothesis. It must pass a human gate and wet-lab gain-of-function (Phase II) before any downstream decision. This report is hypothesis generation, never a decision.
