§5 · Direct answer

The RAG back-end is categorized on two orthogonal axes — the 13 RAG data-niches (RN1–RN13, artifact/data-type, from Indices_nichos) and the 6 scientific-domain niches (N1–N6, PROJECT_SCOPE) — fed by the 9 authoritative databases (Bases de datos) via a provenance-tagged crosswalk. The enriched scheme adds a 5-axis tag model, an entity layer bound to the verified-identifier store, section-first chunking, a first-class RN10 interaction table, and a deterministic-then-semantic retrieval router. The team-biomedic's proposal to merge ocular into biophysics is rejected (unanimous composite-audit): the biomedic conflates the shared-physics-law axis (already the RN axis) with the scientific-domain axis; merging would collapse Test 5. Two amendments accepted (metabolic discriminator; ocular morphology already = RN9). The taxonomy decision is queued for a human gate; additive enrichments were applied without reorganizing the taxonomy.

The enriched categorization scheme (the "idea")

5-axis tag model (per corpus item)

  • AXIS-1 data_niche [RN1–RN13] — primary + secondary (multi-label, cross-referenced, not duplicated)
  • AXIS-2 scientific_domain [N1–N6] — orthogonal subject filter (keeps N5 ocular distinct → Test 5)
  • AXIS-3 source_db + provenance [physical > genetic > curated > computed > predicted] — STRING(predicted) never stands alone
  • AXIS-4 file_type → modality — extension narrows, keyword ranks (a .csv maps to 9 niches → modality+role, not extension, is the key)
  • AXIS-5 bio_context — stage_hpf, tissue, segment, species/taxon

Entity layer (bound to the DATA INAMOVIBLE — never mint IDs)

Every gene symbol resolves via resolve(): hit → ENSDARG + tier · positive NOT_FOUND → kept, conf 0 · absent → QUARANTINE (UNVERIFIED) until Ensembl lookup + raw-cache + human gate. Ontology xrefs: ZFIN, GO, Reactome, PSI-MI, MeSH, ZFA.

RN10 promoted to a first-class interaction table

rag_index/interaction_table.json — Source → Target → Signal → Temporal-window, queryable on 4 keys; the chaperone-tissue backbone; consumed by RN13 Morpheus models as boundary conditions; unknown rates surface as gap_flags (visible, not silently zero).
